Parts acquisition and PCB production can be done through any supplier or partner that an end users chooses. While we do not endorse or support exclusive use of any manufacture or vendor, we provide information below detailing how we initially handled parts acquisition:

PCBs and the majority of parts were initially acquired through JLCPCB (https://jlcpcb.com/). The zipped folder containing the Gerber files can be uploaded to generate the PCB. The bom (and cpl) file contained in this subfolder can be uploaded to JLCPCB to generate an orderor available parts. Parts that were not available through JLC were purchased using Digi-key (https://www.digikey.com/).

Another option used for initial parts ordering by one of our teams was LCSC (https://www.lcsc.com/). Again, parts that were not available were acquired via Digi-key. NOTE: the "1Meg" component is a 1 mega Ohm (1Mohm) resistor with a 0603 footprint. LCSC appears to not register this part but it can be found by searching manually for these specifications. For example searching for 1M ohm 0603 will give you parts that work, including Part number: RC0603FR-071ML from YAGEO.
